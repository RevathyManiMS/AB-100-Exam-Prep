How to use this site

A three-pass approach works well if you have roughly a week.

PassWhat to do
FirstRead all eleven study pages in order. Do not memorise — aim to recognise the vocabulary and the decision points.
SecondTake the mock exam in practice mode, one module at a time. After each module, re-read the study page sections you got wrong.
ThirdTake the full 110-question exam mode run under time. Anything below 70% in a module sends you back to that page. Read the cram sheet the morning of the exam.

Study guide by topic

One page per module, each with key concepts, design guidance, exam traps, and a readiness checklist.

MODULE 1 · Q1–Q10 Introduction to agentic AI business solutions Align AI solutions with business goals, know the Microsoft AI stack, and recognise which out-of-the-box agent already solves the problem. MODULE 2 · Q11–Q20 Analyze requirements for AI-powered business solutions Decide where agents genuinely add value, then prove the underlying data is fit to ground them. MODULE 3 · Q21–Q30 Design overall AI strategy for business solutions Use the Cloud Adoption Framework to build an enterprise AI strategy, and decide when to extend Copilot versus build custom. MODULE 4 · Q31–Q40 Evaluate costs and benefits of AI solutions Build a defensible ROI case, decide build versus buy versus extend, and control the variable cost of generative AI. MODULE 5 · Q41–Q50 Design AI agents for business solutions Design task, autonomous, and prompt-driven agents in Copilot Studio, plus Copilot customisations across Dynamics 365. MODULE 6 · Q51–Q60 Design extensibility of AI solutions Extend Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio with connectors, MCP, Computer Use, and Foundry custom models. MODULE 7 · Q61–Q70 Orchestrate configuration of prebuilt agents and apps Enable, configure, and orchestrate the AI already shipped in Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 before building anything new. MODULE 8 · Q71–Q80 Monitor, analyze, and tune AI agents Instrument agents, read the telemetry, and run a continuous improvement loop that keeps quality rising. MODULE 9 · Q81–Q90 Manage testing AI-powered business solutions Test probabilistic systems properly: evaluation metrics, validation criteria, adversarial testing, and quality gates. MODULE 10 · Q91–Q100 Design ALM process for AI-powered business solutions Move agents, connectors, data, and models through environments safely, repeatably, and auditably. MODULE 11 · Q101–Q110 Design responsible AI security, governance, risk management, and compliance Apply the Responsible AI principles, defend against prompt manipulation, and design access control, residency, and audit.
