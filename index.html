How to use this site

A three-pass approach works well if you have roughly a week.

PassWhat to do
FirstWork through the eleven modules in order. Watch the matching video episode or read the Learn module, then read the study page here. Do not memorise — aim to recognise the vocabulary and the decision points.
SecondTake the mock exam in practice mode, one module at a time. After each module, re-read the study page sections you got wrong.
ThirdTake the full 110-question exam mode run under time. Anything below 70% in a module sends you back to that page. Read the topic information and the cram sheet the morning of the exam.
