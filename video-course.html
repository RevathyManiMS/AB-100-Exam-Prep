The AB-100 video course

Microsoft Learn publishes a free, instructor-led video course for AB-100 on its YouTube channel. It is 16 episodes and 9 hours 27 minutes in total, and it follows the eleven modules of the Learn path in order — so it lines up one-to-one with the study pages on this site.

This is the one part of this site that is genuine Microsoft material. Everything else here is an unofficial revision aid. If you only have time for one source, use this one.

How to combine the video course with this site

The episodes explain; the study pages compress; the mock exam tests. Used in that order they take roughly a week.

StepWhat to do
WatchOne module's episodes per sitting — most are around 45 minutes. Start with the 6 min course preview to see whether the pacing suits you.
CompressImmediately afterwards, read that module's study page. It restates the same material as concepts, design guidance, and traps — which is what turns a watched video into a recallable one.
TestRun that module's questions in the mock exam in practice mode. Anything you miss points you back at a specific section.
ReviseIn the final days, skip the videos and use the cram sheet and topic information instead. Re-watching nine hours is not revision.
